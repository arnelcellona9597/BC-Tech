The Monogram

A serif B held inside an open C. The red slash in the opening stands for a code “/” and a pen nib, so the mark covers both design and development.

Business tagline

Built to Bring You Business.

Plain words any business owner understands in one read: we build your website, and the website brings you customers.

Logo files

Each layout comes in four versions, as SVG and PNG. The checkered tiles show the transparent files: they have no background, so they sit on any colour or photo.

Full lockup

Logo, name and tagline. Use on the website footer, documents, social banners.

Horizontal + tagline

Icon, name and tagline in one line. Use in the website header, email signatures, invoices.

Horizontal (no tagline)

For small spaces where the tagline would be too small to read, such as a mobile menu bar.

Icon

The mark alone. Use for social profile pictures, app icons, stamps.
