About BC Tech

A small team that cares about your sales

BC Tech is a new company, but our team is not new. Each of us has more than 7 years of experience in web design and development, and we are based right here in Davao City.

Our story

We started BC Tech to make websites that pay for themselves

After years of building websites for many kinds of businesses, we saw the same problem again and again: a good-looking website that did not bring in customers.

So we built a company around a simple idea. Start with what you want more of, whether that is calls, bookings or online orders, and design everything to get you there.

Our first client, LYFD Stainless Fabrication in Davao City, gave us the chance to prove it. See how it went.
