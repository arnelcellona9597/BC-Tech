Questions

Quick answers

How much does a website cost?

A simple website of up to 6 pages starts at ₱25,000. Online stores start at ₱70,000. Bigger projects with custom features are quoted after a free call. See the pricing page for every package.

Can I edit the website myself?

Yes. Our CMS websites let you change text, photos and products yourself, with no coding. We show you how.

I already use a certain platform. Can you work with it?

Yes. WordPress, Shopify, Laravel, React, Next.js and more. If you prefer a certain tool, tell us. We can handle it :)

Can you connect my website to my other software?

Yes. We can link your website to your accounting, inventory, payment or ERP system so your team stops typing the same thing twice.

Will people find my website on Google?

We set up every page so Google can understand it: clear titles and descriptions, fast loading and mobile-friendly design. No one can promise the number one spot, but you will start strong.

What do I pay for besides the website?

A domain name (your web address) and hosting (where your website lives). Monthly care and a growth plan are optional. All are listed on the pricing page.
